Why Louisiana

The proof starts in Baton Rouge. The assets are statewide.

BRYC anchors implementation, but the pathway data, aid rules, colleges, apprenticeships, credentials, and workforce signals are Louisiana-wide.

Baton Rouge as proof site. Louisiana as the student-success opportunity surface.
What is already built

The scarce part exists

  • Canonical student profile with academic, family, career, preference, and narrative context.
  • Recommendation engine for institutions, programs, scholarships, apprenticeships, and short-term programs.
  • Advisor review, customization, publication, and student-facing recommendation links.
  • Transcript/GPA processing, meeting capture, and transcript-analysis review flows.
  • Event-sourced audit trail for profile changes, recommendations, edits, and advisor decisions.

Statewide data spine

Louisiana-specific pathway intelligence

  • Louisiana program catalog with embeddings.
  • CareerOneStop Louisiana workforce data.
  • CIP-SOC crosswalk for education-to-career mapping.
  • Louisiana occupation wages and career outcome signals.
  • Scholarship, apprenticeship, and short-term credential recommendations.
Trust model

Human advisors stay in control

  • AI generates the pool; advisors decide what becomes guidance.
  • Advisors can customize selections and add local knowledge.
  • Transcript analysis suggests updates; advisors accept or reject.
  • Every meaningful action becomes implementation evidence.
The honest gap

PSSG funds the missing persistence layer

1. Better-fit pathway recommendation
2. Advisor-reviewed plan
3. Application, acceptance, enrollment
4. Persistence, credits, transfer, credential, career
Evaluation-ready

The event trail is the evaluator's friend

  • Which recommendation was generated.
  • What the advisor changed before publication.
  • What the student received.
  • Which follow-ups, cases, and interventions occurred.
  • Which outcomes followed over time.
What to add

The proposal should fund five buildouts

  • Outcome tracking: enrollment, credits, retention, transfer, completion.
  • Advisor follow-up workflows after high-school graduation.
  • Recommendation feedback: edit/remove reason and student choice reason.
  • Postsecondary and workforce data-sharing partnerships.
  • Independent evaluation aligned to early-phase evidence building.
Bottom line
BRYC has the pathway engine. PSSG funds the persistence engine.

The case is not speculative software. It is Louisiana student-success infrastructure growing out of a live proof site.
